# Phase 9F-C3 Prestige Shop product decision

**Status:** DEFERRED for the V2 launch

**Reason:** Pending live/beta player feedback and a defined Prestige-specific reward catalog.

The absence of the first-party Prestige Shop does not block Phase 9F completion, final acceptance, release
qualification, or the V2 launch. Phase 9F-C3 intentionally adds no Shop button, GUI, command, permission,
configuration section, purchase service, persistence table, price, discount, or dormant placeholder.

The accepted architecture already keeps generic exact-decimal currency, canonical actions and transactions, and
revision-bound administration separate from progression. Those boundaries leave room for a future Shop without a
speculative abstraction now. Future design starts only after player evidence defines the rewards, Prestige gates,
currency and cost model, purchase limits, persistence, and audit requirements.

The next planned phase is **Phase 9F-D — final GUI polish and usability**.
